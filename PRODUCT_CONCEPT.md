# Quillspark — Product Concept & Feature Specification

## Product concept

**Quillspark is an AI writing partner for the messy middle between a rough thought and a piece of writing worth sharing.** It helps people get unstuck, find the right words, and shape drafts without taking over their voice.

Quillspark is designed for writers who want momentum and control—not a blank-page replacement. Users bring the context, intention, and point of view; Quillspark supplies options, structure, and editorial lift.

### Positioning

> **From rough thought to right words.**

Unlike generic chat interfaces, Quillspark is organized around the writing workflow: capture an idea, build a useful first pass, refine with visible controls, and export a polished result. Every suggestion is optional, traceable, and easy to undo.

### Primary audiences

- **Marketers** who need campaign copy, briefs, landing-page language, and channel variations quickly.
- **Students** who need help turning notes into clear explanations while keeping ownership of the work.
- **Professionals** who want sharper emails, reports, proposals, and executive communication.
- **Creative writers** who want prompts, alternate lines, scene expansion, and a patient collaborator.

### Product principles

1. **Momentum over magic:** help users make the next useful move.
2. **Voice stays yours:** preserve intent and style; never overwrite silently.
3. **Visible control:** make tone, length, structure, and audience adjustable.
4. **Responsible assistance:** encourage review, attribution, and fact-checking where it matters.

## Core workflow

1. **Bring context** — paste a rough note, prompt, outline, or source material and describe the outcome.
2. **Shape together** — choose a task, audience, tone, and length; review suggestions in-place.
3. **Send with confidence** — accept, revise, compare, copy, or export the finished draft.

## Feature specification

### MVP: writing workspace

**Input and context**

- Multiline prompt composer with placeholder guidance.
- Optional context fields: audience, goal, tone, and desired length.
- Support for pasted notes and outline-style input.
- Clear distinction between user-provided context and generated suggestions.

**Generation modes**

- Brainstorm ideas.
- Draft from notes.
- Rewrite for clarity.
- Summarize.
- Expand with examples or supporting detail.
- Proofread for grammar and flow.
- Adjust tone: clear, warm, direct, persuasive, playful, or formal.

**Refinement controls**

- Make shorter / make longer.
- Simplify language.
- Add specificity.
- Change tone.
- Create 3 alternate versions.
- Compare original and revised text.
- Undo / redo and restore previous versions.

**Output actions**

- Copy to clipboard.
- Export as plain text or Markdown.
- Save a draft to a workspace.
- Keep a lightweight revision history.

### Trust, privacy, and responsible AI

- User owns their writing and can delete workspace data.
- Clear indicator when content is AI-generated or AI-assisted.
- No silent training claim in product copy; explain data handling in plain language.
- Encourage users to verify facts, citations, and sensitive claims.
- Preserve source context and show what changed in rewrite mode.

### Growth roadmap

- Brand voice profiles for teams.
- Shared workspaces with comments and approvals.
- Document import and export integrations.
- Source-aware writing with citations.
- Multilingual drafting and localization.
- Personal writing patterns and reusable prompt recipes.

## Success measures

- Time from first prompt to a usable first draft.
- Percentage of generated suggestions accepted or edited.
- Repeat weekly writing sessions.
- Export/copy completion rate.
- User-reported confidence and reduction in blank-page time.
